## Change and evidence boundary

Names are recovered from their original completed publication jobs. Creation,
previews/tasks and removed tasks have separate tabs. Owners can remove/restore
terminal tasks individually or move the visible completed previews as a batch.

failed outcomes or someone else's tasks. Classifications and exclusion details
remain available inside collapsed reports.

one ordered fresh `run_started_native` and `run_ended_native` provide boundaries;

is a valid terminal. Recorder Close alone is not a terminal. Strict sequence proof
and research selection are unchanged; a complete game is not a zero-error recording
or a scientifically admitted dataset. Old profiles refresh once in the existing
bounded CPU worker, without publishing a new dataset or retrying uncertain actions.
